Privacy Policy

WisdomScroll · Last updated 5 November 2026

WisdomScroll is a mobile app that shows short passages of classic philosophy. This page explains what data the app handles, why, and how you can delete it. We’ve kept it short and in plain language.

In short:

  • You can use WisdomScroll without an account.
  • We store only what the app needs: your likes and saves and, if you sign in, your name and email address.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking, and we never sell your data.
  • You can delete your account and all of its data at any time, in the app.

Who is responsible

WisdomScroll is run by ScrollWisdoms (Peter Francek, Slovakia), who is the controller of your personal data. Contact: scrollwisdoms@gmail.com.

What we collect, and why

DataWhenWhy
A random account ID The first time you like or save a quote So your likes and saves are kept, even before you sign in
Your likes and saves (which quotes, and when) When you like or save a quote To show your saved quotes and keep them across phones
Name, email address and Google account ID Only if you sign in with Google To let you sign in and recover your account on a new phone
Technical data: IP address, device and app version, time of requests When the app talks to our server Our hosting provider records these to keep the service secure and working

Kept only on your phone and never sent to us: the themes you pick, and what the app learns about what you like (which quotes you read for longer or skip), which it uses to choose your next quotes. Deleting the app removes it.

Daily quote notifications, if you turn them on, are scheduled by the app on your phone. We don’t send them from a server and don’t receive anything when they appear.

Read aloud uses your phone’s own speech engine. The quote text is spoken by your phone; we don’t receive any audio.

AI reflections shown under quotes are written in advance for each quote, not for you: nothing about you is ever sent to an AI service.

Legal basis

We process your likes, saves and account details to provide the app’s features you ask for (performance of a contract, GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)). Technical data is processed for our legitimate interest in running a secure, working service (Art. 6(1)(f)).

Who processes data for us

  • Supabase hosts our database and sign-in. Your data is stored on servers in the EU (Ireland).
  • Google handles “Continue with Google”. Google’s own privacy policy applies to your Google account.

Both are bound by data-processing agreements. Both are US-based companies; where data is accessed from outside the EU, it’s protected by the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses or the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

How long we keep it

  • Signed-in accounts: until you delete the account.
  • Without signing in: your likes and saves are deleted automatically if you don’t open the app for 90 days. (Without an account, we have no way to give them back after you uninstall the app anyway.)
  • Technical logs: kept by our hosting provider for a short period, then deleted.

Delete your account

In the app:

  1. Tap the person icon at the top right of the feed.
  2. Tap Delete account, then confirm.

Your account, likes and saves are deleted from our server immediately, and the app on your phone is reset.

No longer have the app? Email scrollwisdoms@gmail.com from the email address of the Google account you signed in with, and we’ll delete the account and all of its data within 30 days.

If you never signed in, there’s nothing to request: your data is tied only to the app on your phone and is deleted after 90 days without use.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your data, and object to or restrict how we process it. Email us at scrollwisdoms@gmail.com. You may also complain to a data-protection authority; in Slovakia, that’s the Úrad na ochranu osobných údajov SR.

Children

WisdomScroll isn’t directed at children under 16, and we don’t knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If we change this policy, we’ll update the date at the top of this page. If a change matters for how your data is used, we’ll also tell you in the app.
